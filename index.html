A weekly reset, without the pressure

You do not need to solve every part of your life on Sunday.

Start by clearing what is taking up space in your head. Check the week you actually have. Choose a few priorities. Then decide what happens next.

The 20-minute sequence

Four small steps. One usable plan.

  1. 01

    Clear your mind

    Write down tasks, reminders, and loose ends so you are not trying to hold them all at once.

  2. 02

    Check commitments

    Look at your calendar, available time, and the energy this particular week can hold.

  3. 03

    Choose priorities

    Pick up to three priorities, identify the first step, and decide what can wait.

  4. 04

    Plan next steps

    Make a simple seven-day action plan that helps the important work move forward.

Questions, answered.

What do I receive?

You receive one digital PDF containing four undated worksheet pages. Nothing physical will be shipped.

Can I type into the PDF?

No. The workbook is designed to print and write on by hand. It does not contain fillable form fields.

What size paper is it for?

It is designed for US Letter paper. If you print on A4, select “Fit to page” in your printer settings.

Can I print it again later?

Yes. It is for personal use, and you can print copies for yourself whenever you need a new weekly reset.
